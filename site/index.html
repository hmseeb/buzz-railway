Four steps, no keys pasted into anything shady.

You make your identity in the app first. Your private key never leaves your device. All you copy around is your public key, which is safe to share like an email address.

1

Install the Buzz app

Grab it for Mac, Windows, or Linux from the releases page, then choose "Create a new identity key." The app makes your account and keeps the private part on your device.

Get the app
2

Copy your public key

In the app, open your profile and copy your public key. It starts with npub. This is the only thing you carry over, and it is safe to paste anywhere.

3

Deploy, paste your key

Click Deploy on Railway. In the owner field, paste your npub. That makes you the owner. Everything else provisions itself, no configuration.

4

Connect the app to it

Railway gives your server a web address. In the app, add a community and point it at that address. You are in, as the owner, with the key that never left your device.

The things people ask first.

Do I need to know how to code? +

To deploy, no. It's a Deploy button and one field where you paste your public key. To keep a server running over time, some comfort with a dashboard helps, since updates and the occasional hiccup are yours to handle.

Where's the chat app? Is this it? +

This deploys the server. You talk to it with the Buzz desktop or mobile app, not a browser tab. Install the app, point it at your server's address, and that's your workspace.

Is my private key ever exposed? +

No. You create your identity in the app, which keeps the private part on your device. You only ever paste your public key, which is safe to share. There is no password reset though, so back up your key from the app.

Can other people join? +

Yes. Your workspace is private by default. From inside the app you create invite links and send them to teammates. They join as members; you stay the owner.

What does it cost? +

Whatever Railway charges for the computing, typically a few dollars a month for a small team, more with heavy use. The software itself is free and open source. You see the bill and control it.
